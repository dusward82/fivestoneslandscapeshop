Shipping Details

We know you’re excited to receive your plants and garden products. Our goal is to make the shipping and delivery process as simple and worry-free as possible.

Shipping Costs

Shipping and handling charges, when applicable, are calculated based on the products in your order, order size, destination, and any special handling requirements. Any applicable shipping or delivery charges will be shown during checkout or confirmed with you before fulfillment.

When Your Order Ships

Once your order leaves Five Stones Landscape, available tracking information and an estimated delivery date will be provided by the shipping carrier when applicable. Please monitor your tracking information so you know when to expect your shipment.

If Your Shipment Is Delayed

Weather, carrier volume, seasonal demand, and other circumstances outside our control can occasionally delay a shipment. Many delayed shipments arrive shortly after the carrier’s original estimated delivery date.

If your shipment is delayed, please review the carrier’s tracking information for the latest update. Once an order has been transferred to the carrier, transportation is handled by that carrier; however, our team is still available to help if you have concerns about your shipment.

Your Plants Are Carefully Prepared

Shipping live plants requires special care. We prepare and package plants to help protect them during transportation and to account for reasonable shipping delays.

When Your Plants Arrive

Unpack live plants promptly after delivery. Carefully remove all packaging and give the plants appropriate water, light, and care based on the needs of the variety you purchased. Prompt attention after delivery helps reduce transplant and shipping stress.

Local Pickup & Delivery

Some products may be available for local pickup or local delivery rather than parcel shipping. Large trees, bulk materials, pottery, fountains, sod, and other oversized or fragile products may have special fulfillment requirements. Available options will be shown at checkout or coordinated with you by Five Stones Landscape.

Questions About Your Shipment?

If you have questions or concerns about shipping, delivery, or receiving your order, please contact Five Stones Landscape at 405-789-4930 or info@fivestoneslandscape.com.

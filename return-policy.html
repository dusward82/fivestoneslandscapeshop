Return Policy

Because many of the products we sell are live plants or other items that can be affected by handling, weather, transportation, and care after purchase, returns require prior approval from Five Stones Landscape.

Please do not return or refuse delivery of plants or other products without first contacting Five Stones Landscape and receiving approval.

Live Plant Returns

We do not accept returns of live plants without prior authorization from Five Stones Landscape. If you have a concern about the condition of a plant, contact us promptly so our team can review the situation and help determine the appropriate next step.

Refused Deliveries

If you refuse delivery of an order without prior approval, you may be responsible for the actual delivery charges incurred to send the order to you as well as any charges required to return the shipment to Five Stones Landscape.

Condition of Returned Products

Any return that has been approved must arrive back in a condition suitable for resale unless Five Stones Landscape has authorized the return because the product was damaged or defective when received.

If a refused or returned product arrives back in a condition that cannot reasonably be resold because of handling, neglect, damage, or delayed return, the customer may also be responsible for the cost of the product.

Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or in an unexpected condition, contact us as soon as possible. Please keep the item and its packaging and, when possible, provide photos so our team can evaluate the issue.

Before Returning an Item

Contact Five Stones Landscape before sending anything back. Unauthorized returns may not qualify for a refund or replacement.

Questions About a Return?

Call 405-789-4930 or email info@fivestoneslandscape.com before returning or refusing delivery of an order.
